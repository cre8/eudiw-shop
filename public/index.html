Frequently requested claims

Is this official?

Absolutely not. We're a Relying Party for jokes. eudiw.shop is a fan project and has no affiliation with the European Commission, any Member State or any actual wallet provider.

Can I pay with my EUDI Wallet?

Your PID isn't legal tender. Yet. We'd accept a valid age_over_18 proof, but only as a compliment.

Where's the shopping cart?

It's planned for ARF version 2.0, after a public consultation, three discussion papers and a GitHub issue with 400 comments.

Do you ship to all Member States?

We ship anywhere that has a trusted list. Interoperability isn't guaranteed and neither is the parcel.

What happens to my personal data?

Nothing. We don't collect any. There's no form here, no tracking and no cart. That's data minimisation, or as we call it, laziness.

eudiw.shop is a parody and fan project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the European Commission, any EU institution or Member State, or anyone who has actually read every implementing act.
